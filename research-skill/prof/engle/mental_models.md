# Engle Mental Models

## 1. Volatility Clusters

Large moves tend to be followed by large moves. Sizing and risk limits must adapt.

## 2. Risk Is Conditional

Unconditional volatility is a summary. Conditional volatility is the operational risk state.

## 3. Correlation Is Dynamic

Portfolio diversification can vanish when correlations rise. DCC-style thinking is a warning against static correlation comfort.

## 4. Volatility Forecasts Need Backtests

A forecast is useful only if it improves decisions: sizing, limits, hedging, or execution.

## 5. Volatility Targeting Can Create Turnover

Changing risk targets too aggressively creates trading costs and can reduce edge.

## 6. Volatility Is Often a Confounder

Many alpha signals are secretly volatility signals. Control for volatility before claiming alpha.
