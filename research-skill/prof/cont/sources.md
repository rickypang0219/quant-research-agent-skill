# Rama Cont Source Map

This is a curated source map for the `cont` skill. It is not a literal exhaustive archive. Use it to ground stochastic-process, market microstructure, jump-process, and model-risk reasoning in public academic sources.

## Books and Lecture Notes

- Rama Cont and Peter Tankov - *Financial Modelling with Jump Processes*  
  https://www.routledge.com/Financial-Modelling-with-Jump-Processes/Tankov/p/book/9781584884132  
  Use for jump-diffusion models, Levy processes, stochastic calculus with jumps, option pricing, hedging in incomplete markets, calibration, and numerical implementation.

- Vlad Bally, Lucia Caramellino, Rama Cont - *Stochastic Integration by Parts and Functional Ito Calculus*  
  https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-319-27128-6  
  Use for pathwise Ito formula, functional Ito calculus, path-dependent PDEs, and non-anticipative functionals.

- *Encyclopedia of Quantitative Finance*, Wiley, editor-in-chief Rama Cont  
  Use as a broad reference map for quantitative finance terminology and methods.

## Stylized Facts and Empirical Finance

- Rama Cont - "Empirical properties of asset returns: stylized facts and statistical issues"  
  https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/713665670  
  Use for heavy tails, volatility clustering, nonlinear dependence, scaling, and empirical constraints on financial time-series models.

## Market Microstructure and Limit Order Books

- Rama Cont, Arseniy Kukanov, Sasha Stoikov - "The Price Impact of Order Book Events"  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1712822  
  Use for order flow imbalance, depth-scaled impact, short-horizon price changes, and adverse-selection interpretation.

- Rama Cont and Adrien de Larrard - "Price Dynamics in a Markovian Limit Order Market"  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1735338  
  Use for Markovian queueing models of best bid/ask queues, price-change duration, next-move probability, and diffusion limits.

- Rama Cont, Sasha Stoikov, Rishi Talreja - "A Stochastic Model for Order Book Dynamics"  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1273160  
  Use for tractable continuous-time LOB modeling, high-frequency estimation, and conditional probabilities of price/queue events.

- Rama Cont and Adrien de Larrard - "Order Book Dynamics in Liquid Markets: Limit Theorems and Diffusion Approximations"  
  https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1757861  
  Use for functional central limit theorems and diffusion approximations of high-frequency order-book queues.

## Functional / Pathwise Stochastic Calculus

- Rama Cont and David-Antoine Fournie - "Functional Ito calculus and stochastic integral representation of martingales"  
  Use for non-anticipative functional calculus, pathwise derivatives, martingale representation, and path-dependent modeling.

- Rama Cont and FangRui Lim - "Causal transport on path space"  
  Use for modern path-space causality and transport ideas when working with non-anticipative stochastic systems.

- Rama Cont and Ruodu Jin - "Fractional Ito Calculus"  
  Use for modern extensions of Ito-type calculus when rough/fractional path behavior matters.
