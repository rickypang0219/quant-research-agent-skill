# Quant ML Source Registry

Use these sources as methodological lenses:

- Andrew Ng - practical ML course material and data-centric AI principles: problem framing, baselines, error analysis, and iteration discipline.
- Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, Jerome Friedman - *The Elements of Statistical Learning*: regularization, bias/variance, tree ensembles, and model evaluation.
- Marcos Lopez de Prado - *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*: purging, embargo, financial labels, sample uniqueness, and backtest overfitting.
- Bailey and Lopez de Prado - Deflated Sharpe Ratio and selection-bias correction.
- Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado, and Zhu - probability of backtest overfitting and combinatorially symmetric cross-validation.
- Andrew Lo - Adaptive Markets Hypothesis: model performance decays as market ecology changes.
- Fama-French, Carhart, Asness-Moskowitz-Pedersen - factor and anomaly baselines that ML alpha must beat or neutralize.

Use official firm material only for research/dev standards:

- Citadel/Citadel Securities: hypothesis-driven research, large datasets, simulation, high-performance engineering.
- Jane Street: strongly typed systems, research tools, trading/risk infrastructure, and tight technology/trading integration.
- Tower Research: market access, research infrastructure, data, compute, risk management, and quant/core engineering feedback loops.
- D. E. Shaw: systematic investing, quantitative tools, human insight, risk/reward discipline, and collaborative research culture.
