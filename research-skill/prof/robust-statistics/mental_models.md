# Robust Statistics Mental Models

## 1. Outliers Are Either Errors or Risk

You must decide which. Treating genuine crashes as errors is dangerous.

## 2. Influence Matters

If one day changes the signal sign or Sharpe, the result is fragile.

## 3. Median Is a Defensive Baseline

Median/MAD often reveals whether mean/std estimates are being hijacked by tails.

## 4. Robust Is Not Risk-Free

Robust methods can underweight rare but real events. Always separately stress tail scenarios.

## 5. Winsorization Is a Research Decision

Document thresholds. Stress results across thresholds. Never tune winsorization to maximize Sharpe.
