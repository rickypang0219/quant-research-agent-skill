## Books

- Steven E. Shreve - *Stochastic Calculus for Finance I: The Binomial Asset Pricing Model*  
  https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-0-387-22527-2  
  Use for no-arbitrage, binomial pricing, state prices, American derivatives, random walk, and discrete-time foundations.

- Steven E. Shreve - *Stochastic Calculus for Finance II: Continuous-Time Models*  
  https://link.springer.com/book/9780387401010  
  Use for Brownian motion, martingales, stochastic calculus, risk-neutral pricing, exotic options, term-structure models, and jump-diffusion extensions.

- Ioannis Karatzas and Steven E. Shreve - *Brownian Motion and Stochastic Calculus*  
  https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4612-0949-2  
  Use for martingales, stopping times, filtrations, Brownian motion, stochastic integration, SDEs, PDE links, and local time.

- Ioannis Karatzas and Steven E. Shreve - *Methods of Mathematical Finance*  
  https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4939-6845-9  
  Use for contingent-claim pricing, optimal consumption/investment, complete and incomplete markets, equilibrium, and constraints.

- Dimitri P. Bertsekas and Steven E. Shreve - *Stochastic Optimal Control: The Discrete-Time Case*  
  Use for dynamic programming, stochastic control foundations, admissible policies, and discrete-time control logic.
