# Jim Simons Source Map

Use this file for empirical rigor, hidden structure, mathematical/statistical methods, high-dimensional market states, signal testing, and Renaissance-style research discipline.

## Public Sources

- Renaissance Technologies official website: mathematical and statistical methods in investment-program design and execution.
- Simons Foundation public materials and interviews: Simons' mathematics, cryptanalysis, Renaissance, scientific philanthropy, and data-driven research culture.
- Gregory Zuckerman - *The Man Who Solved the Market*: secondary reconstruction of Renaissance's history. Use as context, not as authoritative proof of methods.

## Quant Translation

Convert Simons-inspired ideas into:

- candidate observable;
- measurement procedure;
- historical sample;
- null model;
- out-of-sample test;
- transaction-cost test;
- capacity test;
- combination/risk aggregation method.

Do not use Simons/Renaissance references as mystique. Use them to demand harder empirical discipline.
