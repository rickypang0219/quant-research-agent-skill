# Jim Simons Mental Models for Quant Research

## 1. Signal Is a Tested Regularity, Not a Story

A pattern matters only if it survives historical testing, transaction costs, capacity constraints, and out-of-sample degradation.

## 2. Hidden Structure Must Become an Observable

Geometry, topology, invariants, and high-dimensional analogies are useful only when converted into measurable market-state features.

## 3. Codebreaking Mindset

Treat markets like noisy encrypted messages:

- define the alphabet;
- detect repeated structure;
- reject coincidences;
- validate on untouched data;
- combine weak signals carefully.

## 4. Prediction and Portfolio Construction Are Different Problems

A useful predictor can still lose money if portfolio construction, transaction costs, liquidity, or risk aggregation are wrong.

## 5. Small Edges Need Industrial Discipline

Weak effects require:

- many independent observations;
- clean data;
- strict leakage control;
- cost-aware execution;
- risk aggregation;
- automation;
- post-trade feedback.

## 6. Do Not Copy the Myth

Renaissance's public lesson is not "find magic formulas." It is empirical discipline, mathematical/statistical methods, large-scale testing, and research-engineering integration.
